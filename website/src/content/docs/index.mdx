<h1 id="ly-title">Labelyard</h1> <p class="ly-tagline">The self-hosted Label Printer Hub for all your labels</p>
<p class="ly-pitch">Labelyard runs in one container next to your printers and gives every label one place to come from. Scan a code on your phone, print from Snipe-IT, Grocy or Spoolman, or send a job from any app that speaks HTTP. Tape and label printers, office printers with label sheets, one print queue that tells you what happened.</p>

<section class="ly-section" aria-labelledby="ly-what">
<h2 id="ly-what">What it prints, and from where</h2>
<dl class="ly-features">
<div><dt>Tape and label printers</dt><dd>Brother PT-Series and QL-Series printers on your network. The hub reads the loaded tape itself, so a label is laid out for what is in the printer.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Office printers and sheets</dt><dd>IPP page printers print onto label sheets. The hub remembers which cells of a sheet are used, so a half-used sheet goes back into the tray.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Scan to print</dt><dd>Install the web app on your phone, scan a barcode or a printed label, and print it again or its neighbour.</dd></div>
<div><dt>One queue for everything</dt><dd>Pause, resume, cancel and retry per printer and per job. Live status pages show each printer without reloading.</dd></div>
<div><dt>API keys and webhooks</dt><dd>A versioned API under /api/v1 with scoped keys per caller, and webhooks that print when something changes in another app.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Snipe-IT, Grocy, Spoolman</dt><dd>Plugins look up assets, products and spools by their ID and print them. Paperless and Kept print through the API.</dd></div>
</dl>
</section>

<section class="ly-start" aria-labelledby="ly-start">
<div>
<h2 id="ly-start">Running in two minutes</h2>
<p>Labelyard needs two values: a secret key for what it stores, and the address you open it at. Everything else, printers included, you set up in the browser. Keep <code>labelyard.env</code>: without the key, stored secrets cannot be read.</p>
<p><a href="/install/">The full installation guide</a> covers reverse proxies, HTTPS and the first sign-in.</p>
</div>
<div>

```bash
echo "LABELYARD_SECRET_KEY=$(openssl rand -base64 32 | tr '+/' '-_')" > labelyard.env
docker run -d --name labelyard -p 8000:8000 \
  --env-file labelyard.env \
  -e LABELYARD_PUBLIC_URL=http://localhost:8000 \
  -v labelyard-data:/data \
  ghcr.io/strausmann/labelyard:1.6.0
```

</div>

The **cart** collects labels before you print them, much like a shopping cart. Use it when you want several labels at once: a shelf of assets, the products of one delivery, a box of spools.

## Filling the cart

Look up or scan an item and choose **Add to cart** instead of printing it right away. The cart button shows how many labels it holds. The cart lives in the browser you use; another device has its own.

## Previews and warnings

On a Brother QL with 62 mm tape the cart shows a preview for each layout in it, not only for the first item. Labels with the same layout look alike, so the cart draws the first item of each one, in the order of the cart. It shows at most four previews and says how many more layouts it leaves out.

<Screenshot shot="cart-check" lang="en" alt="The cart with previews of two layouts and a warning on an item whose QR code does not fit the tape." />

Once the printer reports its tape, the cart also checks every item against that tape and the chosen arrangement, without printing anything. An item that cannot be drawn gets a red note under its name, for example **The QR code does not fit this tape: 812 of at most 122 bytes.** Above the print button, the cart says how many items will not print.

The warning does not stop you: the print button stays on. Remove the item, pick another arrangement or a shorter link, then check again; or print anyway and the hub refuses only the items that cannot be drawn. If the check itself fails or is busy, the cart says **Could not check the items; the print still checks them.** and shows no warnings. The print always checks every item again.

## Printing the cart

Open the cart and print everything in it:

- **On a tape printer** the labels go into the printer's queue together, as one batch (see [print queue](/using/queue/)).
- **Onto a sheet** the preview places the labels on the free cells. What does not fit stays in the cart for the next sheet (see [sheets](/using/sheets/)).

If an item was deleted in its app since you added it, the hub cannot print it and tells you. Remove it from the cart and print again.

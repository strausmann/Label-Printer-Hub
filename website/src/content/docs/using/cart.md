---
title: Cart and bulk printing
description: Collect labels from several lookups and print them together, on tape or onto a sheet.
---

The **cart** collects labels before you print them, much like a shopping cart. Use it when you want several labels at once: a shelf of assets, the products of one delivery, a box of spools.

## Filling the cart

Look up or scan an item and choose **Add to cart** instead of printing it right away. The cart button shows how many labels it holds. The cart lives in the browser you use; another device has its own.

## Printing the cart

Open the cart and print everything in it:

- **On a tape printer** the labels go into the printer's queue together, as one batch (see [print queue](/using/queue/)).
- **Onto a sheet** the preview places the labels on the free cells. What does not fit stays in the cart for the next sheet (see [sheets](/using/sheets/)).

If an item was deleted in its app since you added it, the hub cannot print it and tells you. Remove it from the cart and print again.

Check pages: three passes on one Avery Zweckform 6121 sheet

Printed black on white at 100 %. Pass 1 prints the full check page. Passes 2 and 3 print only their own crosses with mini scales and one short run line, on crossings that never touch the earlier ones, so one sheet serves three prints.

Mockup notes (not part of the app)

  • Spec 7.2 and the comments of #240: margins only, an 8 mm QR and the number in every cell, inner 0.9 mm and outer 1.8 mm corner marks, page codes in spots, check passes K1 to K3 on one 6121 sheet (maintainer, 2026-11-15).
  • All codes are real: gen_qr.py encodes the LYC1 payloads of section 7.2 in alphanumeric mode (page 5-M, cell 1-M). No payload contains @, ://, mailto:, tel:, sms:, WIFI:, geo: or BEGIN: (checked when the codes are generated).
  • Operator decision, 2026-11-21: pass 1 is the full check page; passes 2 and 3 print only their K2/K3 crosses with mini scales and one run line each (run number, date and time, pass, correction applied yes/no), pass 2 in the bottom-left slot, pass 3 in the bottom-right slot of pass 1's margin. No cell numbers, no codes and no margin block on passes 2 and 3.
  • Printable area (spec 7.2): every element of every pass lies inside it, assuming a 5 mm unprintable edge on the office laser. Nothing is printed above 5 mm or below 292 mm, and the dashed orange line marks the edge in the overlay and the strips.
  • Page-code spots (spec 7.2): no fixed corner squares. Each page code is a 10 mm symbol (5-M, 0.270 mm modules) in the first spot inside the printable area and clear of every cell element. On a 6121 with a 5 mm edge the page corner is too tight (top margin 10.8 mm, left 9.9 mm), so the codes land in cells 1, 5, 61 and 65 at the cell's outer corner, inset 2.5 mm; the number of those cells moves to the free inner corner of the cell (my call: the horizontally inner corner on the same edge row as the page code, because the vertically inner corner holds the K1 cross); their own centred 8 mm cell codes stay.
  • Strokes are drawn 0.13 mm instead of 0.1 mm so they survive the screenshot; the pass pattern beyond pass 1 is my call (section 17 leaves it to the builder): passes never share or touch a crossing, diagonals included, and the build fails if they do.
  • Demo values: run numbers 12 to 14, printer "Office laser", correction dx +0.41 mm, dy −0.66 mm.
